**Cryptid Castle** is an ADGA Registered Nubian Dairy Goat farm owned by **Kelly Kleidon**, located in **Toledo, Washington** — established in 2021.

We are passionate about raising quality Nubians with excellent conformation, strong dairy character, and wonderful personalities. Our goats are shown at ADGA sanctioned shows throughout Washington State, including the **Washington State Fair in Puyallup**, where we earned **First Place for Educational Display in 2025**. 🏆
